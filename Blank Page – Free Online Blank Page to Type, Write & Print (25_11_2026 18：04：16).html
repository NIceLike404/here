How to delete a blank page in Word?

To delete a blank page in Microsoft Word, click at the end of the content on the page before the blank page, then press the Delete key until the blank page disappears. You can also enable paragraph marks (Ctrl+Shift+8) to reveal hidden characters or page breaks, then select and delete them. This removes any unwanted blank pages from your Word document.

How to remove a blank page in Word?

To remove a blank page in Word, place your cursor at the top of the unwanted blank page, then press Ctrl+Shift+End to select all content on it, and press Backspace or Delete. If the blank page persists, turn on formatting marks (Ctrl+Shift+8) and delete any extra paragraph marks or manual page breaks that are forcing the extra page.

How to delete a blank page in Google Docs?

To delete a blank page in Google Docs, click at the very beginning of the blank page and press Backspace until it is removed. If the page remains, check for extra spacing under Format → Paragraph styles → Normal text and reduce the spacing. You can also click at the bottom of the previous page and press Delete to remove a page break that may be causing the blank page.
